# FH Statement Validity: Closing the Specification Gap

**Status:** Draft 0.1 · The elephant, addressed. The kernel checks *proofs*; nothing checks that a *statement* means the mathematics it claims to formalize. This document defines the program's answer: a defense-in-depth battery (`atlas vet`), its integration as an agent feedback loop, and the publication gate it feeds.

## 1. The problem, sized honestly

The 2026 research state is blunt. Recent measurement found roughly one in five formalizations by state-of-the-art autoformalizers on graduate-level analysis carries at least one faithfulness error, and the failure taxonomy is exactly what we'd fear: silently weakened hypotheses that become usable-but-wrong lemmas downstream, and quantifier inversions that turn deep claims into trivial facts — while the standard benchmarks measure proving *conditional on the statement being correct*, making the upstream error invisible. Equivalence-checking studies find barely half of verified cross-prover statement translations preserve meaning under definitional-equivalence tests. And the position-paper consensus states the structural fact plainly: unlike proofs, no automated system can verify translation correctness with high certainty — the informal↔formal correspondence is informal at the root. Our own Phase 0 added the sharpest possible exhibit: for "energy stability of plane Couette flow," *the informal literature itself* disagrees about what the words mean (admissible-class dependence, 20.65 vs 44.3). The gap is real, measured, and — decisively for us — **it is a taxonomy of specific failure modes, each of which can be individually attacked.** Certainty is unavailable; engineered error rates and auditability are not.

## 2. Two structural mitigations we already own

**The TCB is the statement file.** Proofs are kernel-checked; the trusted surface is definitions + frozen statements, kept small, and — FH's founding bet, now revealed as a *validity* feature, not a convenience — kept **readable**. A Rust-literate physicist can review `theorem couette_energy_stable_below(Re: Rat, h: Re <= LOWER) -> energyStableAt(Re)` against the paper in a way no elaborated Lean term allows. Statement review is human work; FH's job is making that work possible at a glance. **Disputes get formalized, not resolved by fiat.** When the informal source is ambiguous (Couette), all readings become named statements and the difference becomes a theorem-level diff — ambiguity surfaced is ambiguity defused.

## 3. The battery: `atlas vet <statement>` — seven probes

Each probe targets a named failure mode; no single probe suffices; the report is itemized and machine-readable.

**V1 · Controls (anti-vacuity, anti-degeneracy).** Positive controls: every hypothesis class ships a *witness certificate* — an explicit inhabitant, proven (an "admissible perturbation field exists" lemma, without which a stability theorem is vacuously true). Negative controls: the statement must certifiably *fail* where the mathematics says it should — `¬ energyStableAt (10^6)` must be provable, else `energyStableAt` is degenerate; the wet-lab positive/negative-control discipline, ported from our validation benchmark to the statement layer. Property-testing sweeps (`#eval`/plausible-style) sanity-check definitions on known instances before any proof effort is spent.

**V2 · Slack detection (the `home` probe).** Run bound-minimization on the statement itself: if hypotheses are deletable or the conclusion trivially strengthenable, the statement is weaker or stranger than intended — the silently-weakened-hypothesis failure mode, detected mechanically by the Atlas operation we built for other reasons. A statement whose minimal form surprises its author is a statement that needs re-reading.

**V3 · Definitional diversity.** Every load-bearing definition gets ≥2 independent formulations (the threshold as variational supremum *and* as spectral condition) with a proven equivalence between them. An error must now be made twice, identically, through different mathematics — and the equivalence proof is precisely the BEq-style check the faithfulness literature uses as its gold metric, run internally as a requirement rather than externally as an audit.

**V4 · Connectivity (semantic pinning).** A statement's meaning is certified by its *position*: it must prove the known corollaries it should imply, follow from the known stronger results that should imply it, and specialize correctly to known instances. The Atlas dependency graph makes this a computable score — and an isolated statement (proves nothing expected, follows from nothing expected) is flagged regardless of how correct it looks, because isolation is what quantifier-inversion errors look like from the graph's perspective.

**V5 · Skeleton conformance.** `atlas similar` against the genre's canonical skeleton: a certified stability threshold should skeleton-match the other certified thresholds; a bracket theorem should match the bracket schema. Shape divergence from genre is not an error but is always a review flag — most historical statement bugs are shape anomalies before they are semantic ones.

**V6 · Round-trip informalization.** Machine-render the formal statement back to English/LaTeX (FH's surface syntax is already half the rendering) and have an independent agent diff the rendering against the source paper's claim — the roundtrip-verification-and-repair framework from the current literature, which reports both that the roundtrip signal detects semantic drift without ground truth and that *diagnosis-guided repair* (localizing which stage drifted) substantially beats blind retry. We adopt both halves: the signal as a probe, the diagnosis as the repair-task generator.

**V7 · Independent double formalization (the strongest single control).** A second agent, given only the informal source — no access to the first formalization — produces its own statement set; the two are then proven equivalent or their diff is surfaced. Divergence means one of three precious things: an agent error (caught), a genuine ambiguity in the informal source (the Couette case — caught *mechanically*, before Phase 0 luck), or a normalization mismatch (units, conventions — the physics classic). Statements are cheap relative to proofs; doubling them is the best-priced insurance in the program.

**Physics riders.** Units/dimensional linting (the reserved `units` attribute becomes load-bearing); nondimensionalization invariance (the statement must transform correctly under the claimed scaling — Reynolds-number conventions being the canonical trap); and calibration gates (instantiated statements must reproduce literature numbers *before* certification effort, per couette-re-e's G2).

## 4. The feedback loop and the gates

`atlas vet` emits a scored, itemized report; every failed probe generates a concrete repair task (diagnosis-guided, per V6's precedent) rather than a verdict; the ledger records report + repairs + re-vet. **Gate integration:** G1 (statement freeze) now *requires* a passing vet with V1, V3, V7 mandatory for flagship claims — the freeze hash covers the statements *and* their control lemmas, so vacuity guards travel with the theorem. Any statement edit re-triggers vet (hash discipline makes this automatic). **The publication gate — the referee pack:** every published result ships (i) the emit-lean artifact (zero FH dependency, round-trip-gated), (ii) the vet report with all controls, (iii) the informalization rendering diffed against the paper's abstract-level claim, (iv) the double-formalization diff or equivalence proof, and (v) for disputed territory, the named-readings appendix. The pack's message to referees: *here is not just the proof, but the audit trail that the proved thing is the claimed thing* — which no informal paper provides and which becomes, itself, a selling point of the genre.

## 5. Honest limits and the ledger

The root correspondence stays informal — the position-paper consensus is correct, and the battery's goal is to beat the measured ~19% baseline by orders of magnitude through independent probes with uncorrelated failure modes, then concentrate the residual risk in one small human-readable file whose review is the community's job and FH's readability mandate. Costs: statement-layer effort roughly doubles (V3+V7); against proof-phase costs this is noise, and against a retraction it is free. First deployment: couette-re-e G1, where V7 would have found the admissible-class ambiguity by construction and where the vet report becomes part of the dispute-adjudication deliverable itself. The elephant, domesticated: not by pretending the gap closes, but by making every known way of falling into it into a probe, a score, and a repair task.

# Part II — The Deep Battery (imported from twenty years of specification validation)

Software verification met this exact problem decades ago — "the proof is correct; is the spec?" — and built mature machinery: vacuity detection and coverage from model checking (formally dual: vacuity mutates the *spec*, coverage mutates the *system*, both re-run verification on mutants), mutation analysis for proof-assistant specs (mCoq; MutDafny; IronSpec's mutation-based spec validation), and the seL4 project's assumption-ledger culture. Part II imports it all.

## 6. V8 — The mutation battery and definitional coverage

Systematically mutate frozen statements — quantifier flips, hypothesis deletions, inequality reversals, constant perturbations, index off-by-ones — and require every mutant to die: either the proof *fails* on the mutant (the clause is load-bearing), or the mutant is separately *refuted* (we prove its negation), or it is flagged as a survivor for mandatory human review. A surviving mutant is exactly a clause the verification never needed — the model-checking literature's definition of vacuous satisfaction, and the empirical record shows the check has teeth: mutation harnesses routinely expose author-labeled "strong" specifications as incomplete (postconditions that check one direction of an intended iff being the canonical catch — precisely our silently-weakened-hypothesis failure mode, caught mechanically). The dual metric, **definitional coverage**: instrument which clauses of each definition the main proofs actually consume; an untouched clause is specification the theorem never validated — a coverage hole, reported per definition. Statement mutation score + definitional coverage become the two numeric axes of statement trust.

## 7. V9 — The discrimination gate

Adapted from the three-gate spec-validation pattern (resolution / vacuity / discrimination): beyond being non-vacuous, the statement must *discriminate* — provably reject deliberately wrong neighbors. For every flagship claim we construct its nearest falsehoods (the threshold claim at a Reynolds number past the transition; the bracket with endpoints swapped; the stability predicate applied to a known-unstable flow) and prove the statement false there. V1's negative controls test the definition; V9 tests the *theorem's precision* — that it separates the truth from the adjacent lies it will be cited against. A statement that cannot be made to fail anywhere nearby is not strong; it is suspect.

## 8. V10 — The junk-value and elaboration audit (the Lean-specific trap)

Mathlib's totality conventions are a faithfulness minefield for physics readers: division by zero yields zero, natural subtraction truncates, square roots of negatives vanish — silent junk values that make a formally-true statement mean something a physicist would never sign. The audit: a linter walks every partial-operation occurrence in the statement layer and demands either an explicit side-condition hypothesis or a ledgered `junk-ok` acknowledgment with justification. Same pass audits elaboration invisibles: which typeclass instances actually resolved (the wrong order or norm instance silently changes a statement's meaning), which coercions fired, and any defeq surprises — all printed into the vet report, because the statement the reader sees and the statement the kernel checked must be certified to be the same statement.

## 9. V11 + V12 — Differential semantics and adequacy

**V11, differential execution:** where definitions are executable, run them — Lean `#eval` on small instances against the *independent* Rust numerics that Phase 2 builds anyway. Two implementations of one meaning, written by different processes in different languages; disagreement is a specification bug or a code bug, and either catch pays. (The codegen round-trip makes this nearly free once checkers are extracted — the same artifact serves both validation directions.) **V12, adequacy theorems:** when the formal definition is a finite proxy for a continuum object (couette-re-e's truncation-supremum definition standing in for the textbook functional), the continuum formulation is stated too and the bridge is proven — or, where the bridge is itself research, the gap is *tagged as a named assumption* rather than left implicit. This is the logical-frameworks tradition's adequacy discipline: the encoding's faithfulness to the encoded object is itself a theorem obligation, never a vibe.

## 10. The social layer: preregistration, upstreaming, and the assumption ledger

**Preregistration.** Publish the frozen statement file, its vet report, and its controls *before* the proof campaign — a statement-RFC window in which the community (and, for disputed territory, the original authors) can object to the spec while proofs run. Science's preregistration culture, imported: the claim of record is fixed before the result exists, which kills both spec-drift and the appearance of it. **Upstreaming as review.** Every definition pushed toward Mathlib buys expert adversarial review as a side effect — the cheapest high-quality spec audit available anywhere — and thins the prelude per the codegen doc's track (c) simultaneously. **The assumption ledger**, seL4's great cultural export: a machine-readable file enumerating everything *not* proven — model assumptions, junk-value acknowledgments, adequacy gaps, instance-resolution notes — shipped in every referee pack. seL4 proved a kernel correct while stating plainly that compiler, hardware, and boot code were assumed; that explicitness is why the proof is trusted. Ours says: here is the theorem, here is its audit trail, and here — itemized — is exactly where believing it still requires believing us. **Redundant-property proving**, their other habit worth stealing: prove extra expected invariants *about* the definitions purely for confidence (seL4 proved alignment and bookkeeping properties beyond functional correctness for exactly this reason) — V4's connectivity requirement, practiced deliberately rather than opportunistically.

## 11. Aggregation

The vet report v2 rolls up: controls (V1), slack (V2), diversity (V3), connectivity (V4), skeleton (V5), round-trip (V6), double-formalization (V7), mutation score (V8), discrimination (V9), junk/elaboration audit (V10), differential checks (V11), adequacy status (V12), plus the assumption ledger — into a single statement-trust dossier per theorem, versioned with the freeze hash, regression-tracked as the corpus grows. G1 mandates V1/V3/V7/V8/V10; publication mandates the full dossier. The load-bearing member, load-tested: every probe has a decades-old precedent, a named failure mode it kills, and a repair task it emits — and the residual, as ever, lives in one small readable file and one honest ledger.

# Part III — The PBT Import: Shrinking the Specification

Property-based testing contributes three ideas beyond Part II's mutation battery, and the deepest one — shrinking — generalizes from test inputs to the statements themselves, ending in a construction that unifies this whole document with the program's oldest pattern.

## 12. Generators as instruments

**12.1 Habitability metering (V1, automated).** PBT generators over the hypothesis classes — random admissible perturbation fields, random models of the axioms — turn witness-finding into a continuous instrument: a generator that produces satisfying instances easily certifies non-vacuity *with a distribution*, while a generator that fails after N structured attempts is a vacuity alarm with a search trace. Generator success rate becomes a per-hypothesis "habitability" metric in the vet report — and Lean's `plausible`/SlimCheck machinery means this runs natively against the actual formal definitions, not a shadow model. **12.2 Metamorphic relations, named.** The physics riders (nondimensionalization invariance, units transformation) are metamorphic properties — *properties of the property*: generate an instance, transform it by a claimed symmetry, require the statement's verdict to transform accordingly. Every symmetry the informal mathematics claims becomes an executable spec-test, and a metamorphic failure localizes a convention bug (the Reynolds-normalization trap) faster than any human read.

## 13. Shrinking, aimed at statements

Shrinking's insight — a failing thing should be *minimized* until every remaining part is load-bearing — applies at three levels, each producing a maximally legible artifact for the agent repair loop:

**13.1 Shrunk counterexamples (standard).** When a control or discrimination check fails, shrink the counterexample to minimal size before reporting: the three-mode perturbation field that violates the claim teaches; the random 400-coefficient one doesn't. **13.2 The minimal load-bearing statement (shrinking against the proof).** Delta-debug the *statement* against its own proof: iteratively delete hypotheses, weaken quantifier scopes, relax constants, re-running the proof each time, until every surviving token is necessary — ddmin with the kernel as the oracle. The output answers the question every formalizer should fear: *what did we actually prove?* If the minimized statement is surprisingly weaker than the frozen one (a hypothesis the proof never needed, a constant that could be anything), V2's slack detection just got proof-guided teeth and a diff-sized report. **13.3 Minimal surviving mutants (shrinking the blind spot).** When a V8 mutant survives, shrink the *mutation*: find the smallest statement change that verification cannot detect. A minimal surviving mutant is a blind-spot certificate — the sharpest possible description of what the current proof-and-control suite cannot see — and small diffs are exactly what diagnosis-guided repair consumes best. The Hypothesis-lineage engineering lesson transfers wholesale: shrink in *grammar space*, not value space — and the M0 syntax→syntax amendment pays a third dividend, since FH surface trees give every statement canonical, always-well-formed shrink positions by construction.

## 14. The statement bracket: sharpness certificates

The construction the imports converge on. Order statements by logical strength; then run the two shrink directions against the two oracles: weaken the statement until it is *maximally strong yet still provable* (13.2, the proof as oracle), and strengthen it until it is *minimally strong yet refutable* (V9's discrimination generators as oracle — tighten each constant, drop each slack, until a counterexample kills it). The result is a two-sided enclosure **in the implication lattice**: the statement of record pinned between the strongest thing we can prove and the weakest neighboring thing we can refute. When the bracket is tight — the frozen statement sits directly against the refutation frontier — we hold a **sharpness certificate**: not only is the theorem true, but its constants cannot be improved and its hypotheses cannot be dropped *within the probed neighborhood*, with the frontier exhibited by shrunk counterexamples. This is the program's signature two-sided-bound pattern — Weil pinning eigenvalues, Hubbard brackets, [LOWER, UPPER] on Re_E — applied to *meaning itself*: the specification bracketed exactly like the quantities it speaks about. A spec with a tight bracket cannot be silently weak (the refutation frontier is adjacent) and cannot be vacuous (the provability side is inhabited); the two classic faithfulness failures are excluded geometrically.

## 15. Integration

Vet report v3 adds: habitability metrics per hypothesis (12.1), metamorphic suite results (12.2), the minimal load-bearing statement diff (13.2, human-review mandatory when nonempty), the minimal-surviving-mutant list (13.3, each one a standing repair task), and the statement bracket with its sharpness margin (14) for flagship claims. Costs are compute, not insight — every instrument here is generate-shrink-recheck loops against oracles we already run — which makes this the rare part of the validity program that scales *with* agent capacity rather than against human attention. The document's arc, complete: Part I made faithfulness reviewable, Part II made it testable, Part III makes it *minimizable and bracketable* — and a specification pinned by a tight bracket, a killed mutant set, full coverage, and a public assumption ledger is as close to "certain before publication" as the informal-formal gap permits anything to be.

# Part IV — Adversarial and Calibrated Validation

Parts I–III are defense: systematic probes a cooperative agent runs on its own work. Part IV adds the two things defense cannot supply — an *adversary* hunting the misreadings the probes' authors didn't imagine, and a *measurement* of the whole battery's actual detection power. Offense, then metrology.

## 16. The faithfulness debate: red team as protocol

The informal↔formal correspondence is the one judgment no kernel makes — which is precisely the shape of problem the scalable-oversight literature built protocols for: structured zero-sum debate lets a weaker judge supervise stronger provers, empirically outperforms single-agent review, and comes with named failure modes and their mitigations. We instantiate it as the **fidelity game**: a Fidelity prover defends "this frozen statement means the paper's claim"; a Divergence prover attacks — and here is why our domain is the protocol's *best case*: every attack must be a machine-checkable artifact. A model where formal and informal verdicts differ; a junk-value scenario the statement silently accepts; a surviving mutant; an instance where the paper's expert would answer differently than the formal predicate. The judge doesn't weigh rhetoric — the exploit compiles and evaluates, or it doesn't. Debate's classic vulnerability (obfuscated arguments burying lies in unverifiable complexity) is structurally mitigated the way the prover-estimator line suggests, by *asymmetry*: the Divergence prover needs one concrete witness (existence claims, cheap to judge), while Fidelity defends globally — and every witness is kernel- or eval-checkable, collapsing judge burden to artifact-checking. Collusion risk (the literature's steganography worry) is handled by independent models, disjoint contexts, artifact-only communication. Scoring is a standing **spec-bug bounty**: Divergence is rewarded per validated exploit, permanently, including post-publication — the security world's disclosure culture imported whole. And the game's second product is armor: the Divergence prover's *citation-threat model* — how will this theorem be misquoted downstream? — becomes each flagship's mandatory **non-claims section** ("what this theorem does NOT say"), the negative abstract that pre-empts the overclaim before a hostile reader finds it.

## 17. Calibration: who vets the vet

The battery's detection power is currently asserted; Part IV measures it. **Fault-injection campaigns**: one sealed agent seeds statement corpora with known bugs drawn from the full taxonomy — weakened hypotheses, quantifier inversions, junk-value traps, convention mismatches, adequacy gaps — blinded; the battery runs; recall and precision are computed *per probe, per failure mode*. Output: detection-rate curves that convert the dossier's "all probes pass" into a calibrated statement — "for failure modes in the seeded taxonomy, the battery's measured recall is r" — with the honest scope note that unknown-unknowns are exactly what §16's adversary exists to convert into known seeds. Three disciplines complete the loop: every bug ever caught (by any probe, any debate, any referee) becomes a **permanent regression seed**, so the battery monotonically accumulates its history; **battery mutation** — disable a probe, re-run the seeds, require misses — confirms each probe is load-bearing (mutation testing of the mutation tester; the method eating its own tail, correctly); and the debate protocol itself gets scored the way the oversight literature scores its games — does our fidelity game measurably advantage truth-telling over exploitation? The 19% literature baseline stops being a comparison point and becomes an axis on a measured curve.

## 18. Triangulation and drift: meaning under version control

**Cross-foundation triangulation.** For flagship statements, re-state in a second foundation (Rocq or Isabelle/HOL — different type theory, different library culture, different elaborator) and check bidirectional equivalence in the ITPEval/BEq style. Formalization errors across foundations are close to uncorrelated — the same misreading rarely survives two elaborators and two communities' conventions — making this the strongest form of definitional diversity available, reserved for the claims that matter most. **Semantic drift monitoring.** The quiet failure Part II's hashes cannot see: a Mathlib bump changes an instance resolution, a notation's meaning, a definitional unfolding — and a statement whose *text* is frozen changes *meaning* under it. The countermeasure: semantic regression — elaborated-term snapshots and instance-resolution records diffed on every dependency update, with any semantic delta triggering automatic re-vet and human review. The freeze hash pins the text; the semantic snapshot pins the meaning; both live in CI, because a theorem's meaning is a dependency-versioned artifact whether we track it or not — Part IV just tracks it.

## 19. The completed stack

Reviewable (I), testable (II), bracketable (III), *attacked and measured* (IV). The final governance note: debate verdicts, calibration curves, and drift events all land in the adjudication ledger with escalation paths to humans, and the referee pack gains its last three exhibits — the fidelity-game transcript with surviving-exploit count (target: zero, with bounty standing), the battery's calibration curve as of publication, and the non-claims section. What Part IV concedes, on the record: adversaries and calibration push the residual risk down and *measure where it sits*; they do not zero it. What it claims: a published FH theorem now carries more independent, quantified, adversarially-tested evidence about *what it means* than any informal paper has ever carried about what it proves — and that inversion, meaning-evidence exceeding proof-evidence being the historical scandal of formalization, is this document finally repairing it.

# Part V — The Corpus Closes the Loop: Trust Composition, Semantic Load, and Divergence as Discovery

Parts I–IV validate a statement. But meaning is not per-statement — it is holistic: every theorem imports definitions, which import definitions, and a flawlessly-vetted theorem over one subtly-wrong inherited definition is wrong all the way down. And Parts I–IV share one more hidden premise: that the informal paper is ground truth. The program's own history refutes that premise — the 2HDM formalization found the *paper* wrong; our Phase 0 found the *literature* self-contradictory. Part V scales validity to the corpus, and then turns the instrument around.

## 20. Trust composition and semantic load

A statement's dossier score is local; its *trust* is inherited. The Atlas dependency graph makes the inheritance computable: every definition and theorem carries its dossier, and trust propagates along import edges — dominated by the weakest link in the transitive closure, weighted by how much of the imported definition the dependent proof actually consumes (Part II's definitional coverage, reused as edge weight). Three instruments fall out. **Semantic load analysis:** rank definitions by transitive dependents — the corpus's load-bearing members in the literal structural-engineering sense — and *allocate validation effort by load*: a definition under ten thousand theorems earns the full Part I–IV battery plus cross-foundation triangulation; a leaf lemma earns the automated tier. Validity economics, made explicit: the battery is expensive, load is measurable, and the multiplication table says heavy validation of shared foundations amortizes across everything above them (this is also why Mathlib-upstreamed definitions are the cheapest trust in the program — their load is carried by an entire community's review). **Weakest-path audits:** for any flagship theorem, compute its minimum-trust dependency path and publish it in the referee pack — the claim's semantic supply chain, with its thinnest point named. **Trust regression:** any dossier downgrade (a surviving exploit, a drift event) propagates *forward* through the graph automatically, flagging every downstream theorem whose inherited trust just dropped — the semantic analogue of a security advisory cascading through a package ecosystem, which is exactly the right mental model: the corpus is a package registry for meaning, and Part V gives it dependency auditing.

## 21. Divergence as discovery: the instrument turned outward

When the fidelity game, double-formalization, or differential execution finds formal↔informal divergence, Parts I–IV assumed our side erred. Sometimes it will be the other side — and the program already holds the two founding exhibits: the 2HDM formalization that invalidated a widely-cited paper's main theorem, and the Couette literature disputing its own sixty-year-old threshold. So Part V adds the **adjudication fork**: every confirmed divergence is classified — *our error* (repair task, regression seed, calibration data), *ambiguity in the source* (named-readings treatment; the Couette path), or *error in the literature* — and the third branch gets a protocol instead of a celebration: independent double-confirmation of the divergence, minimal-counterexample extraction (Part III's shrinking, aimed at the paper), a courtesy-contact window with the original authors before publicity, and publication as a certified erratum — the finding stated with the same referee-pack rigor as any theorem, verdict-free, dependency-diff'd, in the formal-referee house style. This reclassification is the document's quiet thesis inversion: the faithfulness battery, built to keep us from embarrassing ourselves, is *symmetric* — it checks a correspondence, and a correspondence can fail on either end. Run at corpus scale against the literature the corpus formalizes, the validity program becomes the most sensitive error-detection instrument mathematics has ever pointed at itself, and "faithfulness failure" becomes, on the third branch, a publishable discovery genre with a growing track record that started before we did.

## 22. The living theorem

Publication is a checkpoint, not an exit. Every published artifact stays under the standing bounty (§16), inside the drift monitor (§18), and in the trust graph (§20) — so a theorem's dossier is a *living document* with a version history: bounty findings append, calibration improvements retro-apply, drift events re-open review, and genuine defects get certified errata with forward propagation to dependents. The referee pack's final form is therefore not a PDF appendix but a **versioned public dossier** — statements, controls, brackets, transcripts, calibration curve, assumption ledger, supply-chain path, non-claims — that any reader can audit at any depth, years later, against the exact toolchain pins that produced it. And the closing move is to give the format away: publish the dossier *specification* itself, so the standard outlives the project. Parts I–V, complete: reviewable, testable, bracketable, attacked-and-measured, and finally *composed, inverted, and kept alive* — validity not as a gate the work passes once, but as the medium the work permanently lives in.
