# FH Convergence: The Process as a Dynamical System

**Status:** Draft 0.1 · The research process itself — vet loops, proof loops, Atlas iterations, calibration campaigns — treated as what it is: a dynamical system with fixed points, Lyapunov functions, contraction certificates, and *provable* divergence. Whether a loop converges is as diagnostic as whether a series does, and the stack already owns every instrument this requires; this document names the theory and adds one tool.

## 1. The loop inventory and its Lyapunov table

Every iterative process in the program, with its natural potential function: the **proof loop** (G4 fix-until-green; potential = sorry count in the targets' closure — already enforced monotone, and the no-progress cutoff in `couette-research.workflow.js` is a divergence detector we built without naming it); the **vet/repair loop** (potential = the aggregated dossier score; hazard = repair oscillation — fixing one probe breaking another); the **statement bracket** (Part III §14; potential = bracket *width* in the implication lattice); the **calibration loop** (§17; potential = per-failure-mode recall); the **fidelity arms race** (§16; observable = exploit discovery rate vs. closure rate); the **resolve/refinement loop** (§8.1 and the Le Verrier engine; potential = the residual's certified magnitude, floored); **Atlas re-clustering** as the corpus grows (observable = cluster-stability indices between successive runs). Design rule extracted from the table: *every loop declares its potential function at birth*, and any loop lacking one is a bug in the loop, not a gap in the theory.

## 2. The certification contraction: the loop that maximizes certainty

The certification problem gets a genuine convergence structure from the statement bracket. Each round of shrinking-against-the-proof (strongest provable weakening) and shrinking-against-refutation (weakest refutable strengthening) produces two monotone sequences in the implication lattice pinching the statement of record — **bracket width is the Lyapunov function of meaning**, and its stabilization is the sharpness certificate. Around it, the **monotone repair policy**: a proposed repair (to a statement, control, or probe) is accepted only if the aggregate dossier score is non-decreasing — oscillation excluded by fiat, and since the mutant set, control set, and probe set are finite per round, ascent through a finite lattice terminates. The composed loop — vet → repair (monotone) → mutate → shrink → bracket-pinch → re-vet — is the certainty-maximizing engine: each pass provably does not lose ground, the bracket cannot widen, and the fixed point is a statement that is simultaneously sharp, non-vacuous, discriminating, and mutation-hardened. This is the answer to "loops that converge on the certification problem": we don't hope the loop settles; we *design its potential function and forbid descent*.

## 3. Proven divergence: the theorems we already scheduled

The striking part of the question — can divergence be *proven* in the meta-mathematics — is already answered affirmatively in three registers. **(a) The correspondence's limitative theorems** (FHC3/FHC4, `fh-correspondence.md`): the Atlas edge-stream provably never stabilizes to the full graph (any r.e. miner misses true edges — non-convergence of Atlas-completeness as a theorem), and statement-equivalence undecidability means the exploit-class stream feeding §17 is *provably inexhaustible*. Consequence for the calibration loop, stated honestly: it converges **per failure-mode class** (each named class closes) while the class-discovery process is provably non-terminating — an arms race with a theorem guaranteeing it never ends, which is not a flaw but the correct shape: certainty grows without bound and without limit-attainment, like a series converging to a sum it never reaches... except here the *sum doesn't exist*, and we can prove that too. **(b) Obstruction certificates are divergence proofs**: the §8 trichotomy's `obstruct` branch (Risch/differential-Galois no-closure) is precisely a certificate that a specific τ-refinement loop cannot converge in the current basis — proven divergence, routed to promotion. **(c) Physics hands us divergence floors**: certified Lyapunov exponents (the chaos component of the Le Verrier floor) are literal proven exponential-divergence rates bounding what *any* refinement loop can achieve on trajectory prediction — nature's own non-convergence certificates, and we already planned to compute them.

## 4. The RG criterion: convergence as the test of reality (an upgrade to §7)

The deepest payoff, and it upgrades the discovered stratum. Treat corpus growth as a flow and ask which Atlas outputs are its **fixed points**: a skeleton cluster, a discovered meta-axiom, a dictionary row that *stabilizes* as the corpus doubles is robust structure — a natural kind, carved at a joint; one that churns under growth is an artifact of incompleteness or overfit. This is renormalization-group logic applied to concepts — fixed points of the coarse-graining flow are the universality classes — and it hands §7 a second acceptance criterion alongside compression: **a discovered meta-axiom must be a fixed point of the discovery process under corpus growth** (re-run the synthesis on the doubled corpus; the axiom must re-emerge). Convergent-under-data = discovered; divergent-under-data = rejected. Divergence of an Atlas query is thereby *diagnostic*, not merely disappointing: it either flags corpus incompleteness (mine more), genuine mathematical wildness (the correspondence's §2 proves some regions of the interpretability order lack stable structure — churn there is *correct*), or a concept that was never real.

## 5. `fh converge`: the monitor

One new tool, mostly assembled from parts: per-loop tracking of the declared potential function, curve-fitting reused wholesale from `fh grade`'s ε-ladder machinery, and a stamp per loop per epoch — `CONVERGING(rate)` / `PLATEAUED` / `OSCILLATING` / `DIVERGING` — with routing rules that make the stamps governance-active: `OSCILLATING` triggers the monotone-policy audit (some repair is descending the potential); `DIVERGING` forks three ways exactly as §4 prescribes — corpus gap (fund mining), proven wildness (cite the theorem, cap the loop), or metric artifact (fix the potential function); `PLATEAUED` below target routes to the kill-criteria review with the plateau value as evidence. The unification is the by-now-familiar one: each loop's potential-over-iterations is an **error field over process time** (site = the iteration index — §2.1 of the meta doc collecting its final instance), and the grade stamps apply to loops as they do to evaluators. Same machinery, pointed at ourselves, which has been the program's signature move since the reflexive meta-axiom: the process that certifies convergence is itself under convergence certification, its divergences are theorems where they can be and stamps where they can't, and the one loop guaranteed never to close — the exploit stream, the edge stream, the map — is the one the correspondence proves *shouldn't*, because a research process that could converge completely would have nothing left to research. Divergence at the limit is what an open frontier looks like from inside the dynamical system. We can prove ours stays open.
