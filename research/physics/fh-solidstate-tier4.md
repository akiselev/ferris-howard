# FH Solid State, Tier 4: The Hard Problems

**Status:** Draft 0.1 · Fourth tier — the moonshot stratum, aimed at the problems condensed matter actually loses sleep over: the Hubbard model, critical exponents, strange metals, and high-temperature superconductivity. Ambition discipline as always: no target claims to *solve* its problem; each claims a certified beachhead inside it, with the beachhead valuable in itself.

## S4.1 — The Hubbard model, bracketed

The 2D Hubbard model is the field's Riemann Hypothesis: simple to state, believed to hold the key to high-Tc superconductivity, unsolved for sixty years. The honest certified program is *two-sided bounds*: variational upper bounds on ground-state energies (any explicit ansatz, certified evaluation) against rigorous lower bounds (Anderson-bound and reflection-positivity techniques, formalized), machine-optimized on both sides to *narrow the bracket* — with every bracket a theorem, tracked as a function of size, filling, and coupling. Where brackets get tight, certified statements about ordering tendencies follow; where they can't be tightened, that resistance is mapped. Companion rungs with proven-theorem status already in the literature and formalization value now: Lieb's theorems (the half-filled attractive/repulsive results, flux phase), Nagaoka ferromagnetism, the Mermin–Wagner exclusions — the Hubbard model's rigorous skeleton, assembled and certified as the frame the brackets hang on. Success metric, stated honestly: not "solve Hubbard" but "make the world's knowledge of Hubbard's ground state a versioned, certified, machine-queryable object that tightens monotonically" — which no amount of unverified QMC delivers, sign problem or no.

## S4.2 — Critical exponents with certificates (the bootstrap dividend)

The 3D Ising exponents govern real critical phenomena in real materials — liquid–gas transitions, uniaxial magnets — and their best modern values come from the conformal bootstrap: positivity carving exclusion islands, computed in floating point. This is A5's universal positivity engine collecting its condensed-matter dividend: rational-certificate extraction for bootstrap exclusion bounds, making "ν lies in [a, b]" a kernel-checked theorem about the 3D Ising universality class. Complement from the lattice side: certified high-temperature series analysis and certified finite-size-scaling inequalities, so the two rigorous routes to the same exponents *cross-check inside one proof system* — a consistency test physics has never been able to run at certificate strength. First milestone is bounded: certify a known 2D bootstrap/exact-result agreement (where Onsager supplies ground truth), then climb to 3D islands.

## S4.3 — Strange metals and bound-shaped physics

The strange-metal mystery has crystallized into *bound conjectures*: Planckian dissipation, transport and chaos bounds (the MSS chaos bound genre), conjectured universal limits phrased as inequalities — which makes the frontier itself inequality-shaped, our native format. Program: formalize the bound statements precisely (an adjudication service in itself — several circulate with mutually incompatible formulations, and the D1-referee treatment of *what the Planckian claim even asserts* would be a genuine contribution to a confused literature); then probe them where finite rigor exists: finite-N SYK is finite-dimensional quantum mechanics — certified spectral and chaos computations at small N test the large-N lore against theorems; solvable transport models give certified check-or-counterexample verdicts per formulation. Realistic upside: not resolving strange metals, but converting a slogan-dense frontier into a lattice of precisely-stated, individually-tested inequalities — the map of which versions of the bounds survive contact with certified models.

## S4.4 — Superconductivity: the rigorous core, then the exclusion program

Mean-field superconductivity is quietly rigorous — the modern mathematical literature (BCS functional analysis: gap equation, critical temperature asymptotics, the BCS-to-Ginzburg–Landau limit) proves real theorems — and none of it is formalized. Stage one: the machine-checked theory of conventional superconductivity — the BCS gap equation's solution theory and Tc criteria as kernel-checked mathematics, a landmark artifact for mathematical physics formalization and the analysis-heavy sibling of B5's φ⁴₂ program (shared muscle: measure theory, variational calculus, operator analysis). Stage two aims the artifact at the field's great open question, honestly: high-Tc is not a theorem away, but an *exclusion program* is — certified incompatibility results ("pairing mechanism class M cannot produce phenomenology set P under hypotheses H"), composing S4.1's Hubbard brackets, S4.2's certified exponents, and stage one's mean-field theorems into the same intersect-the-certified-regions geometry as Round 5's D2∩D3 flagship. The deliverable is the high-Tc debate's *constraint structure* made explicit and checked: which proposed mechanisms are actually consistent with which established facts, as theorems with named hypotheses — the formal referee, walking into the most contested room in condensed matter.
