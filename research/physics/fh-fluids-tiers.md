# FH Fluid Dynamics: Four Tiers

**Status:** Draft 0.1 · One document, four tiers, same discipline as the solid-state set.
**Field-level footholds.** Fluids is where computer-assisted proof scored its decade-defining win — the Chen–Hou finite-time Euler blowup proof runs on rigorous validated numerics — so the culture already accepts our genre at the highest level. The field's rigorous frontier is inequality- and certificate-shaped throughout: stability is quadratic-form positivity, turbulence bounds are variational problems, and regularity criteria are monitorable inequalities. Mathlib's PDE layer is thin, so statement-level formalization carries more of the early load here than anywhere else in the program — priced in below.

## Tier 1 — Corpus and exact ground truth

Corpus groups: conservation-law derivations at statement level (Euler/Navier–Stokes in velocity and vorticity form, energy/enstrophy identities); the exact-solution zoo (Couette, Poiseuille, Taylor–Green, Burgers via Cole–Hopf) formalized end-to-end — each is calculus, and "this explicit field solves NS" is a checkable theorem the field has never bothered to kernel-check; 1D conservation-law theory (Burgers shocks, entropy conditions) where the mathematics is complete and formalizable; and certified spectral computation infrastructure (Fourier/Chebyshev with interval coefficients — the fluid analogue of Tier-1 solid state's certified spectra, and the substrate for everything above it). Contribution headline: the exact-solution library, certified — small, permanent, and the calibration set for every later tier.

## Tier 2 — Stability, certified

Hydrodynamic stability is eigenvalue analysis of explicit operators: certified Orr–Sommerfeld spectra (interval eigenvalue enclosures for plane Poiseuille/Couette at given Reynolds number — the field's classic numbers, currently trusted numerics, become theorems); and the energy method, which is *literally* quadratic-form positivity — certified energy-stability Reynolds thresholds are the positivity engine's fluid-dynamics debut. Deliverables: kernel-checked critical-Reynolds bounds for the canonical flows, and the first certified instability result (an enclosed unstable eigenvalue is a proof of linear instability — rigorous numerics doing physics adjudication). This tier is bounded, valuable, and structurally identical to work the pipeline already knows how to do.

## Tier 3 — Turbulence bounds and the SOS frontier

The rigorous theory of turbulence is a *bounding* theory: the background method (Doering–Constantin) and its modern auxiliary-functional/sum-of-squares formulation (Chernyshenko–Goulart genre) produce upper bounds on dissipation, Nusselt numbers, and mean quantities by solving convex problems — SDP/SOS with floating-point certificates, i.e., exactly the rational-certificate-extraction territory of A5/D3. Program: certified background-method bounds for Couette and Rayleigh–Bénard (the field's benchmark scalings, with the constants finally proven); certified SOS bound optimization where machine search improves the auxiliary functional and the kernel checks the result; and statement-level formalization of the weak-solution/Onsager layer (energy conservation thresholds) as the corpus's bridge to the analysis frontier. Payoff framing: turbulence's *provable* content is bounds, the bounds are convex, and convex certificates are our native export.

## Tier 4 — The singularity program (the honest Clay path)

The Navier–Stokes millennium problem will not fall to this pipeline; the *methodology that is currently advancing on it* is precisely ours. Three tracks: (i) formalize/verify the computer-assisted blowup genre — the validated-numerics cores of Euler-blowup-type proofs (self-similar profiles, spectral enclosures for linearized operators) re-checked at kernel strength, upgrading the field's biggest recent results from "rigorous numerics, trusted implementation" to "kernel-checked," which the authors of that literature themselves would welcome; (ii) certified singularity search — machine hunts for approximately self-similar candidate profiles in NS-adjacent models (where the search is heuristic and only the resulting enclosure needs certification), plus certified *no-gos* excluding profile classes; (iii) the **certified blowup detector** — Beale–Kato–Majda and Ladyzhenskaya–Prodi–Serrin regularity criteria as kernel-checked *monitors* wrapped around simulations, so a numerical solution carries a running theorem "no singularity has occurred up to time T given monitored quantities" — the theorems-with-lab-handles pattern, with the lab being every CFD cluster on earth. Ledger: (iii) is startable after Tier 1's spectral infrastructure; (i) is a collaboration-shaped season of work; (ii) is the open-ended research arm whose partial outputs are the no-gos.
