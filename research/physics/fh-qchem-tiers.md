# FH Quantum Chemistry: Four Tiers

**Status:** Draft 0.1 · One document, four tiers.
**Field-level footholds.** Quantum chemistry is the largest practical consumer on the whole subfield menu — an entire discipline that runs on approximate energies with *statistical* error bars and has never possessed error bars that are theorems. Its mathematics is finite-dimensional to an unusual degree (basis-set methods are matrix algebra), its lower-bound theory (2-RDM/N-representability) is an SDP hierarchy — another positivity-engine consumer — and its benchmark culture means certified reference values get adopted the week they exist. The through-line of all four tiers: **brackets** — certified two-sided energy bounds, tightened until they decide chemistry.

## Tier 1 — Corpus and certified Hartree–Fock

Corpus groups: the exactly solvable anchors (hydrogen atom, H₂⁺ at statement level, harmonic-oscillator basis machinery — the Q12 skeleton again); Born–Oppenheimer as a statement-level regime declaration (a preview of the meta-axioms program: an effective theory with an error bound, stated honestly); molecular symmetry via point groups (directly consuming the solid-state census machinery); and Hartree–Fock as finite mathematics — the SCF equations, Roothaan formulation, and certified small-basis HF energies (interval linear algebra over explicitly-integrated Gaussian basis integrals; the integrals themselves are closed-form and certifiable). Contribution headline: certified HF/STO-3G energies for the first-row diatomics — modest numbers, but the first molecular energies in history that are theorems, and the calibration substrate for every tier above.

## Tier 2 — Few-electron brackets that close

Helium is the field's hydrogen-of-many-body: variational calculations reach twelve digits, and — decisively for us — *lower*-bound machinery exists (Temple, Lehmann, and modern refinements), so upper and lower certified bounds genuinely close. Program: formalize the variational principle and Temple-class lower bounds; certify explicitly-correlated (Hylleraas-type) upper bounds with interval evaluation; deliver two-sided kernel-checked enclosures for He, Li, H₂ ground states, tight enough to be citable reference values. This tier overlaps the AMO track deliberately — these enclosures feed precision-physics error budgets — and it establishes the bracket pattern in the one regime where it visibly *converges*, before Tier 3 takes it where convergence is the research question.

## Tier 3 — The RDM engine: molecular lower bounds at scale

The reduced-density-matrix method is the field's structural gift to this pipeline: ground-state energy lower bounds from semidefinite programming over 2-RDMs constrained by N-representability conditions (P, Q, G, and the T-conditions) — a positivity hierarchy in exact analogy to NPA, with the same certification path: rational certificate extraction from SDP solutions, kernel-checked. Program: formalize the N-representability conditions and the variational-RDM bound theorem; build certified-RDM lower bounds paired with certified variational upper bounds into the standard product — **the molecular bracket**: [E_low, E_high], both sides theorems, for molecules at fixed basis; plus certified basis-set-truncation inequalities where the (harder, thinner) rigorous literature allows, moving brackets toward the complete-basis limit honestly. Research edge: machine search over which N-representability conditions tighten which chemical systems — currently folklore, becomes a mapped, certified trade-off surface.

## Tier 4 — Certified chemistry and the strong-correlation wall

Two summits. First, **chemical accuracy, proven**: for small reaction systems, brackets tight enough that reaction energetics and barrier *orderings* are theorems — "this pathway is lower, kernel-checked" — the first chemistry predictions in history carrying proofs rather than error statistics; every case where certified brackets flatly contradict a standard method's published number is a service the benchmark culture will amplify. Second, the strong-correlation wall, faced honestly: systems like FeMoco — the poster child of intractability — will not be bracketed by brute force, and this is precisely where the track hands off to the meta-axioms program: certified *effective* descriptions (active-space reductions, tensor-network compressions) with a-posteriori error functionals, so that "we solved a smaller model" comes with a theorem about how far the smaller model can be from the real one. Companion adjudication target: certified quantum-advantage accounting for chemistry — composing Round 4's certified simulation costs with this track's certified classical brackets, so the perpetual "will quantum computers beat classical chemistry" debate acquires, for specific molecules, a certified ledger on both sides. Ledger: Tier 1–2 are startable and self-contained; Tier 3 is the year-scale engine build; Tier 4's first summit is incremental atop it, and its second is the door into the meta-effective program — which is where this document deliberately ends, because the wall it names is the wall that program exists to climb.
