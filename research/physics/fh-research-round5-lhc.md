# FH Research, Round 5: The LHC Track — Certified Inference at the Frontier

**Status:** Draft 0.1 · Extends Rounds 1–4.
**The reframe that makes this honest.** FH cannot make an anomaly real or fake — no formal system can. But look at what frontier "mysteries" actually *are*, operationally: an anomaly is a statistical statement (a p-value, a combination, a look-elsewhere correction) about a comparison between a measurement chain and a theory prediction that is itself a massive exact-algebra computation with a composed error budget. Every layer of that sandwich except the collisions themselves is mathematics — finite, checkable, and currently verified by nobody. The muon g−2 saga is the round's founding parable: the celebrated tension moved decisively not because the experiment changed but because the *theory prediction's error budget was re-adjudicated* (data-driven vs. lattice hadronic vacuum polarization) — the anomaly's fate was decided in exactly the layer FH can formalize. Round 5's wager: at the frontier, mysteries are statistical and algebraic before they are physical, and certifying the first two layers is how you find out what's genuinely left in the third.

## D1 — The certified statistical referee

Anomaly claims live and die by inference procedures that are finite mathematics applied under time pressure: profile likelihoods, look-elsewhere corrections, measurement combinations with correlated systematics. Program: formalize the core inference toolkit (likelihood-ratio asymptotics, trial-factor corrections, BLUE-style combinations) and re-run the frontier's contested adjudications as kernel-checked computations. Two flagship case studies, chosen because the tension *is* the methodology: the W-mass situation, where one measurement famously disagrees with the others and the meaning of any "world average" is exactly a combination-procedure question — a certified combination with every correlation assumption a named hypothesis (B4's referee genre, aimed at experiment); and the g−2 theory budget, where formalizing the SM prediction's composition — hundreds of contributions, two incompatible HVP methodologies as explicit alternative hypothesis sets — produces the dependency-diff that a decade of workshop politics approximated in prose. Deliverable format inherited from B4: dependency graphs, never verdicts. First milestone: one published combination reproduced with certificates and its assumption-sensitivity mapped.

## D2 — Certified SMEFT inference

The frontier's actual engine for "is there new physics in the data" is the SMEFT global fit: thousands of measurements constraining Wilson coefficients through what is, at working order, linear-and-quadratic algebra over a big covariance structure. That's certifiable territory end to end: verified likelihood construction, kernel-checked exclusion regions (convex geometry — certificates, not contour plots), and — scientifically juiciest — *certified flat directions and degeneracies*: theorems that specified coefficient combinations are unconstrained by a given dataset, which is precisely the information that tells experimenters what to measure next and currently circulates as fit-lore rather than results. The pipeline addition is small (the convex/SDP machinery already exists for T4/A5); the cultural addition is large: exclusion plots that are theorems, with stated hypotheses, that a rival fit group can audit by rechecking a certificate instead of rebuilding a code stack.

## D3 — The EFT-hedron, certified (consistency as the deepest probe)

The most profound version of "probing inconsistency at the frontier" isn't statistics — it's the discovery that causality, unitarity, and analyticity carve *positivity bounds* into Wilson-coefficient space: whole regions of imaginable new physics are inconsistent with quantum field theory having a sensible UV completion, before any collision is examined. This is the positive-geometry/moment-problem genre (the EFT-hedron), which means it is the **fourth consumer of A5's universal positivity engine** — the same skeleton as Weil, NPA, and the bootstrap, now bounding the Standard Model's possible deviations. Program: rational-certificate extraction for the known two-to-two positivity bounds; certified exclusion statements ("coefficient region R is incompatible with unitarity+causality," kernel-checked); then the composition that neither community has built — D2's *experimental* exclusions intersected with D3's *consistency* exclusions in one certified geometry, so that "allowed new physics" means allowed by data *and* by the axioms of QFT simultaneously, with both boundaries carrying proofs. If an anomaly ever hardens, this intersection is the first thing everyone will want and the last thing anyone will trust uncertified.

## D4 — Certified amplitudes (the service layer everything rests on)

Every anomaly comparison depends on SM predictions computed through heroic exact algebra — integration-by-parts reductions over millions of terms, master integrals, multiloop symbol manipulations — performed by bespoke code with no independent verification culture. Program: kernel-checked verification of published multiloop reductions and amplitude identities (verification is vastly cheaper than discovery — the certificates check; the CAS-scale search that found them doesn't need to be trusted); formalization of the amplitudes toolbox's discrete backbone (color algebra, spinor-helicity identities, and the amplituhedron's combinatorial-geometric statements, which are finite mathematics wearing physics clothing and a genuinely beautiful formalization target); and the standing bounty implicit in the approach — a single confirmed error found in a load-bearing published amplitude would, by itself, justify the program to the precision community. Fits the round's theme exactly: the "prediction" side of every anomaly is a tower of unverified exact computation, and FH's native trade is making exact computation carry certificates.

## D5 — The flavor census (discrete symmetries, exhaustively)

The flavor puzzle — why the fermion masses and mixings have their bizarre pattern — has a large model-building literature built on *finite group theory*: discrete flavor symmetries (A₄, S₄, Δ(27), ...) predicting mixing textures, scanned by hand, paper by paper, for decades. Finite groups, finite representation assignments, finite predictions: this is certified-exhaustion territory, corpus-native (the math side owns finite group theory). Program: formalize the model-space grammar (group × representation assignment × breaking pattern → predicted mixing texture), then certified exhaustive scans within declared bounds — "within this model-space box, exactly these textures are achievable, and the observed pattern arises only from these assignments" as theorems. The census converts a sprawling literature into a queryable, certified object, catches the inevitable missed cases and duplicated claims, and — the Atlas dividend — exposes the model space to `similar`/`frontier` queries against the rest of the corpus, where discrete flavor groups meeting our 𝔽_p and MTC machinery is unexplored by construction.

## D6 — Neutrino-sector degeneracy theorems

Neutrino oscillation physics — the one frontier sector with guaranteed new-physics content already in hand — runs on exact finite-dimensional algebra (PMNS unitarity, three-flavor oscillation formulae, matter effects) whose *parameter degeneracies* (octant, hierarchy, CP-phase ambiguities) determine what current and next-generation experiments can and cannot resolve. These degeneracies are algebraic facts treated as fit folklore. Program: certified degeneracy classifications — theorems of the form "measurement sets M cannot distinguish parameter regions P₁, P₂ under exact three-flavor evolution" — plus certified breaking conditions (which added measurement resolves which ambiguity). Directly decision-relevant for the DUNE-era program, entirely finite, and a clean demonstration that "certified" can mean "tells experimentalists something they act on."

## Round-5 honesty ledger

The round proves nothing about nature and everything about inference: D1/D2 certify the statistics anomalies are made of, D3 certifies the consistency boundary they must respect, D4 certifies the predictions they are measured against, D5/D6 certify the discrete model spaces they are interpreted in. Ordering: D1's combination milestone and D4's first verified reduction are startable and self-contained; D2→D3 compose into the round's flagship intersection; D5/D6 are steady-state censuses. Standing rule, doubled in force here because this round touches active scientific disputes involving real collaborations: dependency graphs, never verdicts; hypotheses named, never ranked; and the pipeline's only editorial position is that certificates are better than trust. If the frontier's mysteries survive certified inference intact — good: then they're real mysteries, and the field will know it's digging in the right place. If any dissolve the way g−2's tension moved — better to learn it from a kernel than from a decade of workshops.
