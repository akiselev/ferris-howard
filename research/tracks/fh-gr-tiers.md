**Status:** Draft 0.1 · The track with the deepest Mathlib foothold (the manifold library) and the widest rigor spectrum — from computations-as-theorems (Schwarzschild) to a decade-scale formalization summit (Penrose) to referee work on cosmology's live tensions.

## Tier 1 — Corpus and the exact metrics
The statement layer over Mathlib's differential geometry: Lorentzian metrics, curvature, the Einstein equations; then the exact-solution zoo as *computations shipped as theorems* — **Schwarzschild verified to solve the vacuum equations** (a finite symbolic computation nobody has kernel-checked; the GR analogue of the certified exact NS solutions), Friedmann–Lemaître–Robertson–Walker with the Friedmann equations derived, plus Killing-vector/symmetry certifications. Headline: the certified metric library — small, permanent, and the substrate for everything above.

## Tier 2 — The classic tests and causal structure, certified
Geodesic machinery over the certified metrics: perihelion precession, light bending, and Shapiro delay as kernel-checked computations with interval-enclosed values — the historical tests of GR carrying proofs a century after carrying photographs; the causal-structure vocabulary (horizons, trapped surfaces, energy conditions) formalized as the statement layer Tier 3 consumes. Bounded, tutorial-grade, and genuinely citable: "the classic tests of general relativity, machine-checked."

## Tier 3 — Singularity theorems and initial data (the formalization summit, laddered)
The Penrose singularity theorem is this track's φ⁴₂-class flagship — a celebrated, self-contained landmark whose formalization would be a milestone for mathematical GR — approached by honest rungs: the Raychaudhuri equation and focusing lemmas first (bounded, largely computational-geometric), then trapped-surface machinery, then the theorem. Parallel rung with independent value: the **constraint equations and certified initial data** — conformal-method solutions verified as genuine initial-data sets, feeding Tier 4's numerical-relativity interface; and the positive-mass theorem at statement level with its proof routes mapped as future targets. Ledger honesty: multi-year, blueprint-driven, and the right community-scale proposal to write once Tier 1–2 demonstrate the track.

## Tier 4 — Gravitational waves and cosmology: the referee arrives
Where GR meets frontier inference, the Round-5 genre transplants whole. **GW parameter-estimation referee**: the inference chain from strain to source parameters is statistics over template models — certified likelihood/posterior computations for benchmark events, prior sensitivity as named-hypothesis diffs. **The Hubble-tension budget**: cosmology's premier "anomaly" is, like g−2, substantially a dispute about error-budget composition across measurement chains — the dependency-graph treatment (which calibration assumptions, which systematics models, where the chains actually disagree) is D1's founding parable at cosmological scale, delivered as graphs, never verdicts. **Certified monitors for numerical relativity**: constraint-violation growth bounds as running theorems wrapped around NR evolutions — the blowup-detector pattern for the Einstein equations, giving waveform catalogs their first proof-carrying error statements. Ledger: T1–T2 startable now and self-contained; T3's first rungs startable, summit proposed-not-assumed; T4's referee items are independent and immediately relevant to live debates. The track's arc mirrors the program's: certify the textbook, ladder toward the landmark, referee the frontier.
