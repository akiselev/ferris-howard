# FH Soft Matter, Packing & Geometry of Materials: Four Tiers

**Status:** Draft 0.1 · The wildcard track written for joy that turns out load-bearing: packing is the literal Flyspeck/Viazovska lineage (formalized-mathematics royalty), rigidity theory is matroid combinatorics (corpus-native), and polymer topology is knot theory (finite and certifiable). The track where the program's mathematical ancestry and its materials-physics future share a document.

## Tier 1 — Corpus and small certified geometry
Hard-sphere/hard-disk configuration statement layer; contact graphs and packing definitions; certified optimal packings for small cases (n disks in a square/circle — a classical computer-assisted literature with known optima, promoted to kernel strength case by case); combinatorial rigidity foundations — Maxwell counting, Laman's theorem, the rigidity matroid — formalized where Mathlib's matroid and graph libraries make it natural. Headline: certified small-packing optimality results plus the rigidity-theory core, both permanent reference material.

## Tier 2 — Jamming and rigidity, certified
The jamming paradigm's mathematical skeleton made checkable: verified pebble-game algorithms (rigidity decision procedures as certified programs — the verified-algorithm deliverable genre), certified first/second-order rigidity of explicit contact networks via the standard stress/motion linear algebra with exact arithmetic, isostaticity counts as theorems for specific packings. Deliverable: a certified rigidity instrument — feed it a contact network, receive a theorem about its mechanical stability class — pointed first at the canonical jammed-packing datasets whose rigidity claims currently rest on floating-point rank computations.

## Tier 3 — Packing bounds (the ancestral homeland)
Kepler is Flyspeck; dimensions 8 and 24 are Viazovska (with formalization efforts in motion in the community); this tier claims the *unfinished middle*: the Cohn–Elkies linear-programming bounds — the machinery behind the great results — formalized as a certified-bound engine, then run: kernel-checked LP/SDP upper bounds on sphere-packing density in the open dimensions (3-adjacent, 4–7, and beyond), certified optimality gaps against best-known lattices, and machine-searched auxiliary functions with certificates where improvement is possible. The positivity engine again — the Cohn–Elkies functional *is* the Weil-positivity shape wearing geometry — and the track's chance at genuinely new mathematics with the program's oldest skeleton.

## Tier 4 — Polymers, knots, and the glassy frontier
**Polymer topology at scale**: knot and link invariants (finite, certifiable — polynomial invariants over explicit diagrams) as a certified pipeline for polymer/DNA topology claims, including certified knot-type identification for simulated chains — replacing heuristic knot-detection in a simulation literature that quietly depends on it. **The glassy frontier, honestly bounded**: no pretense of solving glass — instead, certified statements in the solvable corner (statement-level formalization of the exactly-solved mean-field spin-glass results, certified computations in finite instances; free-energy brackets for explicit small disordered systems) plus the referee treatment of the replica-adjacent claims that the field itself flags as heuristic — rigor *tags* doing their load-bearing work in the program's most rigor-stratified field. Stretch: certified bounds for random packing observables in finite systems, where "random close packing" — a concept famously without a rigorous definition — gets the definitional-referee treatment: the candidate formalizations stated, their inequivalence exhibited. Ledger: T1–T2 startable now, cheap, and instrument-shaped; T3 is the program returning to its formalized-mathematics ancestry with research intent; T4 splits into an immediate pipeline (knots) and a frontier held honest by tags.
