# FH Nuclear Structure: Four Tiers

**Status:** Draft 0.1 · The track where every theorem is conditional on a modeled interaction — which, far from disqualifying it, makes it the program's cleanest showcase for rigor tags and stated-Hamiltonian honesty: nuclear physics has always known its Hamiltonians are effective; this track makes the knowing formal.

## Tier 1 — Corpus and the deuteron
Isospin/angular-momentum algebra (SU(2) machinery, Clebsch–Gordan — finite representation theory, corpus-native and shared with the flavor census); the shell-model statement layer (configuration spaces, second quantization on finite bases); and the track's hydrogen: **the deuteron with explicit model potentials, bracketed** — certified two-sided binding-energy bounds for stated square-well/Gaussian/one-boson-exchange-class potentials, the bracket pattern at its smallest nuclear instance, with every theorem's conditionality (`given Hamiltonian H_model`) explicit in the statement from day one.

## Tier 2 — Few-nucleon brackets
The helium pattern at mass three and four: certified variational upper bounds against Temple-class lower bounds for triton and alpha-particle binding with stated interactions, on certified finite bases with truncation lemmas (the tail-bound genre, hyperspherical/oscillator-basis flavored); certified effective-range expansions for two-body scattering (low-energy parameters as enclosed values — the quantities the whole EFT tower is fitted to, carrying proofs). Deliverable: few-body reference brackets, explicitly interaction-tagged — and the first place the program's certificates enter a field whose *inputs* are themselves negotiated.

## Tier 3 — The truncation referee (chiral EFT's error bars, adjudicated)
Modern nuclear theory runs on chiral effective field theory with order-by-order truncation-uncertainty estimates — and the community itself actively debates how those error bars should be assigned (Bayesian truncation models, regulator dependence, power-counting disputes). This is D1/AMO-referee territory with unusual openness from the field: formalize the truncation-uncertainty *frameworks* as explicit statistical models, run competing prescriptions as named-hypothesis sets over benchmark observables, deliver the dependency-diffs. Companion certified layer: shell-model diagonalizations at fixed interaction as certified eigenvalue enclosures (the by-now-standard machinery), so the "method error vs. interaction error" decomposition — the field's perennial entanglement — gets a checked boundary for the first time.

## Tier 4 — Certified ab initio and the astrophysical handoff
**Certified no-core brackets**: two-sided kernel-checked enclosures for light nuclei (A ≤ 6-ish) at stated chiral interactions and model spaces, composed with Tier 3's truncation models into the full honest statement — "observable X ∈ [a,b] given interaction I and space S, kernel-checked; extrapolation and interaction uncertainty per framework F, stated" — the (error, regime, cost) triple with nuclear-physics regime clauses. **The astrophysical handoff**: nuclear inputs to r-process and stellar-burning networks (rates, masses near stability) currently propagate with informal error models; certified inputs — even for a handful of anchor reactions — give astrophysical sensitivity studies their first proof-carrying links, the theorems-with-lab-handles pattern where the lab is a neutron-star merger. Honest ceiling, stated as the track's identity rather than its apology: no theorem here is about nuclei simpliciter — every result is conditional on a stated model, and the track's contribution is making that conditionality *machine-readable*, which is precisely what the rigor-tag layer and the Effective Calculus were built for. Ledger: T1–T2 startable and bounded; T3 meets the field where it is already asking for help; T4 is the multi-year composition. The track's meta-role: proof that the program degrades gracefully — where certainty about nature is unavailable, certified conditional structure still compounds.
