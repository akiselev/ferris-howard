## Tier 4 — Certified inverse design
Photonics' inverse-design boom ships structures with simulated performance; this tier ships them with *proven* performance: interval-verified frequency-domain solves certifying a specific fabricated-geometry's transmission/field targets within tolerance, composed with Tier 3's limit certificates into the full design story — "this structure achieves Y, provably; no structure in its class exceeds Z, provably; the design is within (Y/Z) of the physical optimum, provably." Stretch: certified guarantees robust over fabrication-tolerance intervals — the theorem quantified over the manufacturing error, which is the certificate a foundry actually wants.
