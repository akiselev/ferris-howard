# FH Program Priority List — High Impact ∧ Low-Hanging, Jointly

**Selection rule:** every entry must be *both* — a genuine headline (first-ever artifact, live-dispute adjudication, engine-founding, or instantly-consumed instrument) *and* genuinely reachable (weeks-to-one-season, existing machinery or one bounded build, known first milestone). Ideas failing either half are listed at the end so the omissions are auditable. Sequencing dividends (what each unlocks) are first-class ranking inputs, because the program's economics are amortization economics.

## Tier 1 — Start now (the first season)

**P1 · Finish couette-re-e (fluids).** The only target with a passed Phase 0, and it double-headlines: *first machine-checked hydrodynamic stability threshold* + kernel-checked adjudication of a live SIAM-published dispute (Joseph 20.65 vs Orr 44.3, admissible-class dependence). Bounded route already specced (rational trial field; LDLᵀ + tail lemma). **Unlocks:** the certified interval-eigenvalue/positivity infrastructure that P4–P6, P8, P11 and the plasma δW transplant all consume — paying for P1 pays for half the list. Effort: the specced 4–6 weeks of phases. This is the priority; everything else queues behind its infrastructure.

**P2 · The EuclideanDomain deletion demo (B1).** Delete the abstraction, provide ℤ and 𝔽_p[x], require the machinery to re-invent it — bounded corpus, known answer key, no new math needed, and the highest thesis-leverage-per-week in the program: success is the first machine re-invention of a load-bearing mathematical abstraction, the founding data point of the §7 discovered stratum, and the demo that makes every Atlas claim credible. **Unlocks:** the anti-unification/synthesis tooling all later mining runs on, plus the Mathlib-refactor-history benchmark suite.

**P3 · Jarzynski + Crooks, kernel-checked (stat mech T2).** For finite Markov chains these are identities provable *nearly verbatim* — plausibly the shortest path in all of physics from zero to "famous modern theorem, formalized first." No prover has them; stochastic thermodynamics is an active experimental field that will cite the artifact immediately. Effort: small-season, mostly Mathlib probability plumbing. **Unlocks:** the finite-Markov corpus that TURs, mixing bounds, and certified coarse-graining (the Effective Calculus's stochastic column) build on.

**P4 · The positivity engine's first rational certificate: NPA level-1 / I3322 (quantum R1).** One bounded artifact — SDP solution → exact rational feasibility certificate → kernel check — founds the engine with the longest consumer list in the program (Bell bounds, bootstrap, EFT-hedron, RDM chemistry, turbulence, photonic limits, packing: ten tracks and counting). Impact is architectural rather than headline-shaped, which is exactly why it ranks here: it is the most-reused artifact on the list. Effort: bounded once P1's exact-arithmetic layer exists.

**P5 · The certified TDVP error monitor (meta program M1).** The projection-error identity is computable on the fly; the a-posteriori-bounds literature is thin; every tensor-network group on earth runs the uncertified version daily. A working monitor is the Effective Calculus's first shipped instrument, the entangled-systems flagship's opening move, and the program's best "instantly consumed by practitioners" bet. Effort: one focused season (identity formalization + interval evaluation over MPS contractions).

## Tier 2 — Queue next (each one season, mostly riding Tier-1 infrastructure)

**P6 · The wallpaper-group census, certified (solid state S1.2).** Seventeen groups, 130-year-old finished mathematics, apparently never machine-checked in full; finite group theory + lattice arithmetic — squarely our muscle. Clean permanent headline; feeds the entire solid-state tower (indicators, band representations) and the 230-group sequel. **P7 · A kernel-checked measurement combination: the W-mass case (D1).** BLUE-style combinations are correlated linear algebra — bounded — and the subject is a live anomaly whose meaning *is* the combination methodology. First certified combination in experimental physics; the referee genre's debut with maximal relevance-per-effort. **P8 · Schwarzschild solves Einstein, kernel-checked (GR T1).** A finite symbolic computation with a century of fame and no formal check. Honest risk flag: Lean differential-geometry computation infrastructure is the one open question — scope the curvature-computation spike first; if the spike is ugly, this slides a tier without shame. **P9 · Anomaly-free U(1) census, first bounded box (C5).** Cubic Diophantine enumeration + certified exhaustion within declared charge bounds — the Standard Model's arithmetic layer meeting the ℤ-corpus; bounded, SM-native, and the program's cleanest particle-physics entry. **P10 · The physlib quantum package (R1 shortlist).** Mermin–GHZ by `decide`, no-cloning + no-communication, general Knill–Laflamme: modest individually, but as a bundle it's the community beachhead — contributed upstream, it buys the program its citizenship in the formalization ecosystem the later flagships will need. **P11 · Helium brackets that close (qchem/AMO T2).** Temple lower + Hylleraas upper, kernel-checked enclosures for He/Li/H₂ — the first spectroscopic reference values carrying proofs; rides P1's interval infrastructure; co-funds two tracks at once. **P12 · The Frauchiger–Renner dependency diff (B4).** Bounded protocol formalization with outsized discourse impact: both published readings as hypothesis sets, the disagreement as a machine diff. Ranked last in this tier only for sociological handling care, not difficulty.

## Deliberately deferred — failing one half of the conjunction

*High impact, not low-hanging:* φ⁴₂ (B5), Hubbard brackets at scale (S4.1), Penrose (GR T3), the full MTC census (B2), the full EFT-hedron∩SMEFT geometry (D2∩D3), solar-system M4 and residual cartography beyond the G-A toy, singularity searches (fluids T4). These are the program's summits; they are explicitly *not* this list's business, and starting them now would be tourism. *Low-hanging, not high impact:* Robertson uncertainty, Fresnel/transfer-matrix certifications, Solov'ev equilibrium checks, Kepler M1 — correct week-one warmups and corpus filler, done en passant inside their tracks, never priority slots.

## The sequencing logic, stated once

P1 builds the exact-arithmetic and eigenvalue-certificate substrate → P4, P6, P8, P11 and the plasma δW transplant become cheap. P2 builds the synthesis tooling → all Atlas mining and the §7 program become live. P3+P5 open the two consumer communities (stochastic thermodynamics, tensor networks) most likely to *use* certificates rather than admire them. P10 buys ecosystem citizenship. After this list: the first summit attempt gets chosen with a track record behind it, not a manifesto.
